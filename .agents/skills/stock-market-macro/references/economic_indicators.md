# Economic Indicators

## Real Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
- **FRED Series ID:** GDPC1 (or A191RL1Q225SBEA for % change)
- **Healthy:** 2% to 4% annualized growth
- **Concerning:** < 1% growth
- **Crisis:** Negative growth (two consecutive quarters = technical recession)
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly

## Unemployment Rate
- **FRED Series ID:** UNRATE
- **Healthy:** 3.5% to 5.0%
- **Concerning:** Rapidly rising from low base, or > 5.5%
- **Crisis:** > 7%
- **Sahm Rule:** Recession likely if 3-month moving avg rises 0.5% above prior 12-month low
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly

## Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- **FRED Series ID:** CPIAUCSL
- **Healthy:** ~2% (Fed target)
- **Concerning:** > 4% (inflation) or < 0% (deflation)
- **Crisis:** > 8%
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly

## Federal Funds Rate
- **FRED Series ID:** FEDFUNDS
- **Effect:** Rising rates compress valuation multiples (hurt Growth/Tech), increase borrowing costs. Falling rates stimulate borrowing (help Real Estate, Growth).
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly

## Yield Curve (10Y minus 2Y)
- **FRED Series ID:** T10Y2Y
- **Normal/Steep:** > 0% (typically 0.5% to 2.0%)
- **Flattening:** Approaching 0%
- **Inverted:** < 0% — historically accurate recession predictor (12-18 months lead)
- **Update Frequency:** Daily
