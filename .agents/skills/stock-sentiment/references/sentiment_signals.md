# Sentiment Signals Reference

## News Sentiment Scoring
The system uses keyword-based NLP with positive/negative word lists to score article polarity.
- **Polarity range**: -1.0 (maximally bearish) to +1.0 (maximally bullish)
- **Bullish threshold**: > 0.25
- **Bearish threshold**: < -0.25

## Pump-and-Dump Detection
Watch for these red flags:
- Sudden spike in social buzz with low market cap stock
- News polarity extremely positive but from unverified sources
- High social buzz + low fundamental scores = potential pump
- Always cross-reference sentiment with fundamentals before acting

## Analyst Biases
- Analysts tend to have a bullish bias (most ratings are Buy/Hold)
- A consensus rating of 3.0 is actually neutral despite being labeled "Hold"
- Pay more attention to rating *changes* than absolute ratings
- Strong Sell ratings are rare and significant when they appear

## Insider Transaction Types
- **Open Market Purchase**: Most significant — insider spending personal money
- **10b5-1 Plan**: Pre-scheduled, less informative about current outlook
- **Option Exercise**: Often tax-related, less meaningful
- **Cluster Buying**: 3+ distinct insiders buying within 30 days — strong signal

## 13F Filing Lag
Institutional 13F filings are reported with a 45-day delay. By the time you see the data, positions may have already changed. Use as directional signal, not precise timing.
