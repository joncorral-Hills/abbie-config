# Marketing Core: Copywriting, Ideas & Social Content

## Overview

Generate professional marketing copy that converts. This skill handles:
1. **Conversion copy**: Emails, landing pages, ads, sales pages
2. **Content marketing**: Blog posts, guides, lead magnets
3. **Social content**: Posts, threads, carousels, hooks
4. **Ideation**: Campaign concepts, messaging frameworks, content calendars

## Core Framework: PAS → DRIP

For any conversion asset, answer these in order:

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
1. **Problem**: What pain does the target feel? (state it clearly)
2. **Agitate**: Why does this problem hurt more than they admit? (make it visceral)
3. **Solution**: What fixes it? (your product/service)

### DRIP (Differentiation, Risk-reversal, Incentive, Proof)
After PAS, add:
1. **Differentiation**: Why THIS solution over alternatives?
2. **Risk-reversal**: What guarantee removes the "what if it doesn't work?" fear?
3. **Incentive**: Why act NOW not later? (scarcity, urgency, bonus)
4. **Proof**: What evidence backs this up? (testimonials, stats, case studies)

## Copy Formats

### Email Sequence (5-email welcome)
| Email | Goal | Subject Line Pattern |
|-------|------|----------------------|
| 1. Welcome | Set expectation, deliver lead magnet | "Your [thing] is here (+ what to expect)" |
| 2. Origin story | Build trust, humanize brand | "The mistake that cost me $X" |
| 3. Value/education | Teach something useful | "The [framework] that changed everything" |
| 4. Soft pitch | Introduce solution | "When [common tactic] stops working..." |
| 5. Direct CTA | Close the sale | "Last chance: [offer] ends tonight" |

### Landing Page Sections
1. **Headline**: One big promise (not a description)
2. **Subhead**: How you deliver the promise
3. **Hero visual**: Product in use, result, or transformation
4. **Problem callout**: "Tired of...?" (PAS - Problem)
5. **Agitation**: "It's not your fault, but..." (PAS - Agitate)
6. **Solution intro**: "Here's what actually works..." (PAS - Solution)
7. **Features → Benefits**: Not "cloud storage" but "never lose a file again"
8. **Social proof**: Testimonials with specific results, not just "great!"
9. **Risk reversal**: Guarantee, refund policy, trial
10. **CTA**: Single action, repeated 2-3x on page
11. **FAQ**: Handle objections before they become exits

### Short-Form Ad Copy (≤150 words)
- **Hook** (first line) → pattern interrupt or curiosity gap
- **Problem** → single-sentence pain point
- **Twist** → unexpected reframe
- **Solution** → what you offer
- **CTA** → specific next step

### Long-Form Sales Page
- Length: 1,000-5,000 words depending on price point
- Rule: The more expensive/complex, the longer the copy
- Structure: PAS → Story → Mechanism → Proof → Offer → FAQ → Guarantee → Urgency → CTA

## Social Content

### Hook Patterns (first 1-2 lines)
| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Contrarian | "Stop doing [common advice]. Here's why." |
| Listicle | "7 things I wish I knew before [topic]" |
| Personal story | "I lost $10K on [mistake]. Here's what I learned." |
| Hot take | "[Popular belief] is dead. Here's what replaced it." |
| Question | "Why do [group] always [do thing]?" |
| How-to | "How I [achieved result] in [timeframe]" |
| Mistake | "The #1 mistake [audience] makes with [topic]" |
| Curiosity | "The real reason [outcome] happens" |

## Content Ideation Engine

### Brainstorming Prompts
1. What myths does your audience believe that hurt them?
2. What are the 10 most common questions you get?
3. What did you believe 5 years ago that you now know is wrong?
4. What's the most controversial opinion you hold in your field?
5. What process do you do that seems obvious to you but surprises clients?
6. What would you teach if you only had 30 seconds?
7. What case study or result are you most proud of?
8. What's the biggest mistake newcomers make?
9. What tool or resource do you use daily that most people don't know about?
10. What trend is coming that your audience should prepare for?

## Voice & Tone Guides

### B2C (Consumer Products)
- Conversational, benefit-forward
- Emotion > logic (but back with proof)
- Short sentences, scannable
- "You" focused

### B2B (Services/Software)
- Professional but not stiff
- ROI and outcome focused
- Credibility signals (logos, stats, titles)
- "We help [specific audience] achieve [specific result]"

### Personal Brand
- Story-driven, opinionated
- Share failures and lessons
- Direct address, first-person
- Polarize intentionally (not everyone should agree)

## Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

| Bad | Why | Fix |
|-----|-----|-----|
| "We're excited to announce..." | Nobody cares about your excitement | Lead with reader benefit |
| Feature lists without benefits | Features describe; benefits sell | Translate every feature |
| Generic CTAs ("Learn more") | Vague = low click-through | Action + benefit ("Get my free guide") |
| Jargon without explanation | Alienates non-experts | Define or replace |
| No urgency/scarcity | "I'll come back later" (they won't) | Reason to act now |
| Weak social proof | "Great service! - Customer" | Specific result + name/title/photo |
| Ignoring objections | Leaves reader with unanswered fears | Address top 3 objections explicitly |
